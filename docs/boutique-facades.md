Six new official address records, total 16 records / 15 brands across 32 research brands. No new OSM identity approved. Historical Dior and temporary Prada remain reference-date review. Address-only sites do not receive invented facade designs.

Eight original local facade previews: LV stepped glass fins, Chanel black stone, VCA openwork, Gucci garden, Celine two glass tones, Loewe green ceramic approximation (vanilla dark prismarine), Burberry plaid glazing, Vacheron brass approximation. All widths/heights, door and window positions and screen patterns are artist estimates for testing only, not surveyed dimensions. These are front panels with real glazing and invisible lights, not entire buildings; no logo/entity or furnishings. A local 4-block-deep schematic must be fitted to verified building facade coordinates before any integration. Do not paste over existing buildings blindly; inspect in a blank test world first. Air is present in the file; WorldEdit paste with -a ignores air. Existing wall backing would need explicit safe removal to make a real open window. Do not claim this batch creates the street automatically.
