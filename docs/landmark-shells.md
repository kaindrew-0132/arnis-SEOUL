# Seoul named landmark shells — first implementation

Trimage (four towers) is a top-tier S+ target; the user explicitly wants to customize its interiors. No downsizing of footprints or heights. Preserve mapped polygons (including curved portions where mapped), mapped roof and building levels. Existing empty storey slabs and invisible lamps remain. First profile: blue vision glass, white vertical corners and slab-edge bands. No furniture. Source: https://tomoon.co.kr/mko/project/project_view.php?category=project&class=plan&idx=370&page=1&searchCate=&searchData=&searchKind=2&searchType=1&searchVal=%ED%8C%90%EA%B5%90 ; photo https://www.hankyung.com/article/2024070549916 . Four towers and maximum 47 floors confirmed; individual surveyed dimensions not confirmed.

Other initial profiles: Lotte World Tower (silver glass and white corner accents), 63 Building (gold glazing), One Bailey (gray glass, light corner strips and dark floor bands). Sources: https://www.kpf.com/project/lotte-world-tower ; https://secc.co.kr/ko/business/portfolio/all/300 . These are initial material treatments, not final detailed building models.

Matching is intentionally conservative: exact tower names, height envelope, Seoul map anchor, no building:part. Actual OSM names/IDs and mapped floor elevations not yet verified; unnamed or part-only buildings fall back to upstream. No blanket matching on coordinates or guessed IDs. Photo/preset facade systems may override procedural walls; disable them in comparison runs. Only empty interior mode. Existing geometry is retained; no invented podium or crown for matched profiles. Real taper, curved wings, Trimage tower-by-tower floor schedules, penthouse layouts, One Bailey skybridges, 63 mechanical levels and actual map match verification are still required. The new profiles do not yet add any of those geometries.
