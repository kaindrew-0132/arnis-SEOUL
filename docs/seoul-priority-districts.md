## Design principles

- Preserve 1:1 scale and mapped footprints and heights.
- Preserve the district's overall skyline, roads, riverside and building arrangement.
- S+: individually modeled facade and usable empty floor spaces, including penthouse where applicable.
- S: individually modeled facade, principal structure and empty floors where appropriate.
- A: preserve recognizable geometry, colors and characteristic facade patterns.
- B: ordinary compact building generation.
- C: simplified small and auxiliary buildings.
- Empty buildings should appear illuminated at night; no Shanghai-style architecture, arbitrary neon or forced media facades.
- The remaining Seoul area is still part of the citywide world; priority districts do not limit generation coverage.

## Owner-directed refinements (2026-11-26)

- Apgujeong/Cheongdam: include all mapped luxury street boutiques, not merely a handful of flagship stores. Prioritize recognizable storefronts, facade forms, signage and nighttime lighting. Build a verified address/footprint inventory; distinguish stand-alone boutiques, shared buildings, department-store concessions and uncompleted developments.
- Warner Cheongdam: prioritize bespoke facade modeling over generic Sangji Kailum complexes. S+ is a proposed grade, pending verification of construction state and geometry.
- Apgujeong Hyundai, Apgujeong Hanyang and Sangji Kailum: compact ordinary generation (B proposed); preserve mapped footprints, arrangement and heights.
- Yongsan Prugio Summit: do not assign S+ based on personal residential preference. Treat it on par with other comparable Hangang-ro mixed-use residential complexes; A is the working proposal for all such complexes, except independently justified landmarks.
- Do not infer exact floor plans or construct planned buildings as completed without deciding a map reference date.
- The Gallery 832 belongs to Yeoksam, not Apgujeong/Cheongdam; verify status before modeling.

## Open decisions

- Final list and grade of each building or complex, including individual tower selection.

- Performance budgets and measured in-game acceptance criteria.
- No district-specific runtime rules are implemented by this document.
